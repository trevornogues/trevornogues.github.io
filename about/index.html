About Trevor Nogués

This site is run by one person. There is no company team behind it.

Who builds it

I'm Trevor Nogués, a software engineer. On nights and weekends I design, build, and support three iOS apps by myself: every screen, every line of code, and every support email.

The apps are Heartlog (a dating journal), Sweetest (ranking sweet treats), and Changeover (a tennis journal). Each one started as a fix for something in my own life.

Where

Seattle, Washington, USA.

Since when

I ship and support the apps as a solo maker. The live App Store products and this site are current work, not a large product company with a founding date on the door.
